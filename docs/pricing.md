# Pricing

Nimbus API offers three plans. All prices are in USD and billed monthly.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is available to any individual or company, including startups. It includes 10,000 API requests per month and a rate limit of 60 requests per minute. Webhooks are limited to 3 endpoints. No credit card is required to sign up.

There is no separate plan or discount program specifically for startups. Startups that outgrow the Free plan move to the Pro plan like any other customer.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $49 per month. It includes 500,000 API requests per month and a rate limit of 600 requests per minute. Requests above the monthly quota are billed as overage at $0.50 per 1,000 requests. Pro accounts can configure up to 25 webhook endpoints.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated annually with the sales team. Enterprise accounts get custom rate limits, unlimited webhook endpoints, mutual TLS authentication, and immediate data purge on request.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month. Downgrading from Pro to Free takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Unused requests do not roll over to the next month.
